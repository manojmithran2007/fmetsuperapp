Platform Architecture

Phase 1 establishes PostgreSQL on Supabase, strict Row Level Security, two distinct profile tables, and token-based backend authorization.

  • 1

    Separate Profile Schemas

    Independent admin_profiles and staff_profiles tables linked to Supabase Auth.

  • 2

    Two-Tier Approval Gate

    Staff accounts require explicit Admin approval before accessing operations.

  • 3

    Complete Design System

    Tokens for Primary, Secondary, Accent, Neutrals, Typography, Spacing, Radius, and Shadows.

Implementation Roadmap

Four disciplined development phases toward full institutional transit operations

Phase 1 (Active)

Foundation & Auth

Database schema, RLS policies, staff registration, approval model, session guards, and design system showcase.

Phase 2

Fleet & Students

Bus CRUD, student directory, bus assignment mappings, and the Admin staff approval dashboard.

Phase 3

Daily Operations

Date-based bus claiming, student attendance tracking, and standing student live recording.

Phase 4

Analytics & Reports

Historical transit reports, attendance audits, bus utilization metrics, and exportable logs.
